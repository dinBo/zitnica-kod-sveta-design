## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dependable, accessible rural commerce aesthetic crafted specifically for agricultural communities, local farmers, and homesteaders. Rooted in regional agricultural tradition yet engineered for contemporary usability, the visual narrative blends the dependability of heritage farm grain stores with crisp digital legibility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Trustworthy & Local:** Reflects generational farming expertise, fair dealing, and community ties.
- **Practical & Direct:** Prioritizes vital logistical information—stock availability, bag sizes, seasonal feeds, working hours, direct phone dialing, and physical store navigation.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Combines rich agricultural greens with harvest gold and soft, tactile linen tones that feel grounded rather than sterile.

### Design Movement
The system follows a **Tactile-Refined Modernism** approach. It avoids cold tech-heavy tropes or excessive abstraction in favor of structural clarity, high-contrast typography, generous touch targets, subtle organic borders, and clear hierarchy that performs reliably under bright outdoor sunlight on mobile displays.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from rural agriculture: deep harvest pine green, sun-ripened wheat gold, fresh seed leaf green, and rich loamy charcoal.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#225838`):** Deep agricultural green serving as the anchor color for prominent headers, primary action buttons, emphasized badges, and structural brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#A68B50`):** Warm wheat gold used for category chips, seasonal accents, price highlights, and agricultural certifications.
- **Tertiary (`#3E7B52`):** Fresh leaf green applied to in-stock indicators, phone call action states, and affirmative feedback badges.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E2822`):** Dark slate charcoal replacing harsh pure black to maximize body copy legibility while maintaining a soft, natural reading comfort.
- **Canvas & Surface:** Built on warm linen and off-white layers (`#FDFCF7` as page base, `#F7F5EE` for cards and contained sections, with `#EDE8DC` for borders and dividers).

### Accessibility & Sunlight Legibility
All text elements maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against their immediate backgrounds. Touch-to-call action targets and location badges are paired with high-contrast foreground glyphs to guarantee effortless outdoors operation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances traditional artisanal authority with rapid mobile utility. 

- **Headlines (Merriweather):** A sturdy slab-serif featuring open counters and robust serifs that recall agricultural gazettes, heritage sack typography, and pastoral dignity. Used for main promotional headers, store section titles, and featured grain category announcements.
- **Body & Interactive Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, geometric humanist sans-serif with tall x-height and generous apertures. It provides crystalline legibility for granular product specs (weights, feed ratios, seed varieties), inventory tables, and interactive UI controls.

All line heights are deliberately relaxed to support multiline Cyrillic and Latin text comfortably without glyph collision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid with robust gutters to ensure product cards, pricing tiers, and contact banners maintain clear separation on every screen size.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Critical contact bars and call-now anchors remain sticky or thumb-accessible.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 1.5rem` and `gutter-md: 1.5rem` for two-column product displays and side-by-side spec sheets.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout capped at `1280px` width, with `margin-lg: 2.5rem` and `gutter-lg: 2rem`.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens are applied to preserve breathing room between dense product attributes. Form fields, quick-order telephone triggers, and address map panels leverage `space-lg` and `space-xl` internally to prevent tap errors in work environments.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic neon glows and hyper-diffused drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **Tonal Stratification** paired with warm, low-intensity ambient earth shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Primary canvas base (`#FDFCF7`).
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** Warm linen surface (`#F7F5EE`) bordered by a subtle 1px earthen perimeter (`#EDE8DC`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards):** Elevated product containers and highlighted feed bags shift with an expanded lift: `0 6px 14px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Direct Actions & Dialogs):** Persistent bottom telephone dials, filter bottom-sheets, and localized direction drawers: `0 12px 28px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(30, 40, 34, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a structured, welcoming curvature (`roundedness: 2`). Standard elements utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corners, while prominent product cards, location callout banners, and modal dialogs adopt `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).

This curvature softens the robust slab-serif typography and dense inventory grids, communicating friendliness and hospitality without feeling toy-like or overly casual. Pill shapes are reserved strictly for high-priority status chips (e.g., "U skladištu" / In Stock) and quick-action dial floating badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Pozovi Prodavnicu" / Call Store):** Deep agricultural green (`#225838`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` radius, minimum height of `48px` with bold typography and prominent telephone icon prefix.
- **Secondary CTA ("Prikaži Lokaciju" / Show on Map):** Harvest wheat gold (`#A68B50`) or warm linen container with a 1.5px solid border in `#225838`, charcoal text, providing immediate navigational guidance.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Flat with underline or soft `#EDE8DC` hover states for downloading seed catalogs and compositional feeding charts.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding. Fresh green (`#3E7B52` at 12% tint) for "Dostupno" (Available), wheat gold tint for "Sezonska ponuda" (Seasonal), and muted charcoal tint for packaging units (e.g., "Džak 25kg", "Džak 50kg").

### Cards & Product Listings
- **Inventory Card:** Contained surface (`#F7F5EE`) with a crisp `#EDE8DC` border. Features an image container for grains/sacks, followed by a Merriweather title, nutrient/composition specs in `body-sm`, and high-contrast pricing formatted with bold prominence.
- **Direct Phone Action Bar:** Floating mobile dock pinned to the bottom of the viewport containing store operating status ("Otvoreno danas do 17h") and a single-tap direct dial trigger.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search & Filter:** Generous `48px` height with an inset icon, warm linen background, and `#225838` active focus ring with a 2px offset. Placeholder text rendered in muted charcoal (`#1E2822` at 60% opacity).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Controls:** Solid `20px` checkboxes with `0.25rem` corners and radios with circular fills. Active state filled with primary green `#225838` and crisp white check marks, providing distinct tactile state feedback.
